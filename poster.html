Airline Satisfaction

A passenger is not a single score. Delay, cabin, and loyalty interact, and a careful feature pass beats a bigger model.

103,904 flightsXGBoostSHAP

Question

  • Which parts of the trip actually move a passenger from neutral to satisfied?
  • The raw survey has missing delays, zero-inflated ratings, and 18 features with VIF above 5.

Method

  • Arrival delay imputed from departure delay (R² 0.93), not a mean fill.
  • Survey block reduced with PCA into cabin comfort, digital experience, and staff service.
  • 23 features selected, then logistic regression, random forest, and XGBoost.

Evidence

0.987ROC-AUC, XGBoost
0.935F1 on the held-out test
0features left above VIF 5

What the result actually says

  • Business class is satisfied 69.4% of the time, Eco 18.6%, Eco Plus 24.6%. Eco Plus sits between them in price and not in satisfaction, so Class is one-hot, not an ordered number.
  • Business travel in business class is 72% satisfied. The same cabin on a personal trip is 12.2%. The interaction is the feature, not the cabin alone.
  • 99.14% of disloyal customers are business travelers. Retention here is a corporate-account problem.
  • SHAP puts online boarding and type of travel at the front of the explanation. The notebook still has those plots saved.

What it does not show

  • This is one airline survey, not a causal claim that changing boarding would lift satisfaction by the SHAP amount.
  • The held-out score is high because the survey answers are already close to the label. It is a strong model of this form, not a forecast of next quarter.

Beside the other attempts

  • Logistic regression, random forest, and XGBoost on the same 23 features. XGBoost is the one reported: ROC-AUC 0.987, F1 0.935.
  • Eighteen features started above VIF 5. After the survey block went through PCA, none did.
  • Arrival delay was imputed from departure delay at R² 0.93, instead of filling the column with the mean.

Stack

Pandas, scikit-learn, XGBoost, SHAP. Train 103,904 rows, test 25,976. Satisfied passengers are 43.3% of the train set.
